Our story

Beauty work, done properly.

Shine & Glow is a boutique salon in Betnoti built around one belief — that bridal-level care and attention should be available for every appointment, not just weddings.

How we started

A small studio, a big focus on detail.

We opened Shine & Glow to give Betnoti a proper beauty studio — one with trained hands, clean tools and honest pricing. What began as bridal make-up for family and friends grew, through word of mouth, into a full studio for hair, skin and make-up.

"Every client leaves the chair looking like the best version of themselves — that's the only standard we hold ourselves to."

How we work

Consultation first, then the chair.

Every appointment starts with a short conversation — about your skin, your hair, the occasion — before we touch a single product. We keep our service list short and clearly priced on purpose, so you always know what you're booking.

Booking is simple too: message us on WhatsApp with the service you want, and we'll confirm your slot directly in the chat.

What guides us

Three things we don't compromise on.

Hygiene

Every tool is sanitised between clients — no exceptions.

Honesty

Clear prices before you book, and honest advice on what will actually suit you.

Craft

We take the time each look needs, rather than rushing to the next appointment.
